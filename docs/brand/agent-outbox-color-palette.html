05 / Guardrails

Keep it unmistakably Agent Outbox

The character comes from restraint: broad neutral fields, precise amber moments, and status color that always means something.

Usage principles

  • 1Let neutrals dominate. Aim for roughly 80% Ink/Paper, 15% supporting neutrals, and 5% Amber in a typical composition.
  • 2Give Amber one job. It identifies the human decision, the primary action, or one key phrase—not all three at once.
  • 3Keep Paper warm. Avoid replacing it with pure white; the warmth is central to the calm, editorial feel.
  • 4Use Green and Red literally. Never use them as decorative campaign colors or competing calls to action.
